# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner of the app, checking their usual Metrovalencia station on a phone. They do not need a transit-planning tool; they need to see the next train in both directions immediately.

## Product Purpose

Show trustworthy upcoming departures for one selected Metrovalencia station, with direction and minutes remaining clear at a glance. Success means opening the installed app and answering “when is my next train?” within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Clean, calm, direct.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic analytics dashboards, route-planner complexity, decorative transit maps, promotional content, and visual noise that competes with departure times.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful train first.
2. Make opposite directions unmistakable.
3. Prefer honest source and freshness states over false certainty.
4. Keep station selection available without obstructing the result.
5. Design for a quick glance while standing or walking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a practical accessible baseline: semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and no information conveyed by color alone.
